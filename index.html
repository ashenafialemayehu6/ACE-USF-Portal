A year of weather in Ethiopia

Typical seasonal guide for the selected campus area

Regional climate pattern

Belg
Feb–May

A shorter rain period in many central, eastern, and southern areas. It can bring warm, changeable days; the timing and amount differ by region.

Kiremt
Jun–Sep

The main rainy season across much of the highlands and western Ethiopia. Southern and southeastern lowlands often follow a different rainfall pattern.

Bega
Oct–Jan

Generally drier in many parts of Ethiopia, with cool highland mornings. Southern and southwestern areas may still have rain.

Season dates and conditions are typical regional patterns, not a forecast. Ethiopia’s altitude and regional rainfall patterns can make nearby areas feel very different.
